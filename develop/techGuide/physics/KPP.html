KPP Boundary Layer Mixing

The K-Profile Parameterization (KPP) of Large et al. (1994) gives the vertical viscosity and diffusivity in the ocean surface boundary layer, and a non-local tracer flux. Omega’s implementation follows that of MPAS-Ocean and is in src/ocn/KPPMix.cpp; its use is described in KPP Boundary Layer Mixing and its options in KPP Boundary Layer Mixing.

What KPP computes

KPP computes:

  • Ocean surface boundary layer depth (OSBLDepth)

  • Vertical viscosity (VertVisc)

  • Vertical diffusivity (VertDiff)

  • Non-local tracer flux profile (VertNonLocalFlux)

It uses a bulk Richardson depth search followed by profile-based coefficient construction. The non-local flux is a standard part of the KPP formulation and is included by default.

Notation

KPP splits the water column at the ocean surface boundary layer (OSBL) depth h. Inside the OSBL, diffusivity and viscosity are prescribed via a cubic shape function scaled by h and a turbulent velocity scale; below it, only interior mixing applies (e.g., shear instability driven mixing).

The OSBL depth is the shallowest d at which Ri_b(d) reaches the critical value; the search is done per cell in computeOSBLDepth, and the crossing depth is refined by a quadratic fit through the three nearest cell-center Ri_b values.

Shape and stability functions

The non-dimensional functions live in src/ocn/KPPConstants.h in namespace OMEGA::KPP. All shape functions take Sigma in [-1,0] and convert to SigmaMu internally, so callers never flip signs:

  • kppShapeMomentum, kppShapeScalar – gradient shapes for viscosity and diffusivity (SimpleShapes)

  • kppShapeMatched – gradient shape that additionally reaches a prescribed value at the OBL base, used by MatchBoth

  • the non-local flux has no shape function of its own; it reuses whichever scalar shape is in effect, scaled by C_s instead of h*w_s

  • kppPhiInvMomentum, kppPhiInvScalar – inverse Monin-Obukhov stability functions phi^-1(zeta)
