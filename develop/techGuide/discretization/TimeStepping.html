Time Stepping

Partly written

This page does not yet describe the forward-backward, RK2, RK4 and split-explicit time steppers. They are to be written here from the time stepping and split time stepping design documents. Progress is tracked in E3SM-Project/Omega#726.

The time steppers are chosen with the options in Time Stepping, and implemented as described in Time stepping and Split Time Stepping.

Split-explicit RK2

Barotropic forcing

The barotropic forcing is the thickness-weighted column mean of the provisional full-step baroclinic velocity divided by the time step. For each wet edge column, with \(h_k\) from MeanPseudoThickEdge, \(u^n_{bcl,k}\) from NormalBclVelCur, \(R_k\) from IterVelocityTend, and split factor \(s\),

\[ F = \frac{s}{\Delta t} \frac{\sum_k h_k (u^n_{bcl,k} + \Delta t R_k)}{\sum_k h_k}. \]

This includes any column mean remaining in the old baroclinic velocity under the current thickness weights.
